# AI-Agent Career Paths: Microsoft (Copilot Studio / Power Platform / Azure AI Foundry / M365 Copilot) vs Salesforce (Agentforce / Data Cloud / Einstein), US, 2026–2031

Research date: 2026-11-09. Scope: comparative and AI-specific analysis only. Raw national posting counts and salary tables for each platform are covered by another researcher. Many sources here are recruiter, consultancy, or vendor blogs; they are labeled. Search snippets were relied on for several items where the primary page was not fetched, and those are noted.

## 1. Emerging job titles, posting growth, and AI-skill premiums

### Takeaway
The best-documented AI-variant growth signal is on the Salesforce side: Agentforce demand rose 63% YoY globally (87% in North America) in 2026 while overall Salesforce demand fell 13%. That is a reallocation inside a shrinking ecosystem, not net growth. Microsoft has no equivalent ecosystem-wide demand report. Copilot Studio roles are visible but small in count, and skew to contract work through IT services and staffing firms. Across both, AI skills carry a documented posting-level premium of about 28% (Lightcast, 2025).

### Cited Findings
**Salesforce / Agentforce**
- Per the 10K 2026 Salesforce Ecosystem Report: global demand for Agentforce expertise grew 63% YoY, 87% in North America and 132% in India. Agentforce's share of Salesforce job listings rose from 5% to 9%, making it one of the six most in-demand Salesforce skills less than two years after launch. 10K measures how often a product appears in listings, not net new roles — [Salesforce Ben, Oct 19 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/agentforce-jobs-are-booming-while-projects-stall-in-pilot/)
- Same report: overall global Salesforce job demand fell 13% YoY in 2026 (it rose 8% the prior year and fell 37% in 2024). Sales Cloud fell 13%, Service Cloud 21%, Marketing Cloud 28%, and Tableau 32%. North America is 50% of global demand, up 3%. AI, data, and specialized industry skills rose while generalist skills dropped. Consulting partners on AppExchange number 4,247, up 14% — [Salesforce Ben, Oct 16 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforce-job-demand-is-down-13-should-you-still-bother-in-2027/)
- 10K data as summarized by secondary sources: Technical Architect demand grew 27% against 4% supply growth, while Developer demand fell 12% and developer supply grew 20%. Not verified against the primary 10K report, and the Salesforce Ben article says the 2026 report omitted supply data, so this may be prior-year data — [Salesforce Ben search snippet](https://www.salesforceben.com/agentforce-jobs-are-booming-while-projects-stall-in-pilot/); [Fastdolphin (recruiter blog)](https://fastdolphin.com/blog/hire-agentforce-developer/)
- Skillenai indexed Agentforce in 317 job postings over the 90 days to September 2026. "Salesforce Developer" is among the titles most likely to list it — [Skillenai](https://skillenai.com/data/skill/agentforce)
- Recruiter claim: only 5 US profiles combine "Agentforce" with an engineer, developer, or architect title. This points to a shortage of people with the label, not of underlying skills. Commercial source, anecdotal — [Refolk](https://www.refolk.ai/blog/salesforce-agentforce-layoffs-2026-agent-builder-sourcing)
- Recruiter placement observation: AI/Agentforce Salesforce developer roles pay $15,000–$35,000 more than comparable non-AI Salesforce developer roles. This is a single recruiter's view, not a survey — [KORE1](https://www.kore1.com/salesforce-developer-salary-guide/)
- Salesforce is actively hiring Forward Deployed Engineers for Agentforce and Data 360. Postings ask for 6+ years of software engineering or technical delivery experience and about 25% travel (25–50% in an Australian posting). The FDE function is led by a VP of Forward Deployed Engineering (Irina Gutman, Dreamforce 2025) — [The Muse posting](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/salesforce/forward-deployed-engineer-agentforce-data-cloud); [ITPro](https://itpro.com/security/salesforce-doubles-down-on-forward-deployed-engineers-in-uk-agentforce-push); [Salesforce+ Dreamforce 2025](https://www.salesforce.com/plus/experience/dreamforce_2025/series/agentforce_at_dreamforce_2025/episode/episode-s1e49)
- The widely cited "Salesforce hiring 1,000" figure refers to a November 2024 plan for about 1,000 salespeople to sell Agentforce, not FDEs. An aggregator's claim of a 1,000-FDE target is unverified — [BNN Bloomberg, Nov 2024](https://bnnbloomberg.ca/business/technology/2024/11/08/salesforce-to-hire-1000-people-for-ai-product-sales-push); [fdepulse (aggregator)](https://fdepulse-com.pages.dev/companies/salesforce/)

**Microsoft / Copilot Studio**
- Copilot Studio developer postings come mainly from IT services firms and staffers: Accenture ("CoPilot Studio – AI Agents Developer"), Cognizant, Experis, and Apex Systems. Postings ask for 2–3+ years of hands-on Copilot Studio or conversational AI experience; Cognizant notes Copilot Studio was "formerly Power Virtual Agents". Example contract rates: $73.68/hr (Experis, Plano TX) and $60–70/hr (senior role) — [Accenture](https://www.accenture.com/us-en/careers/jobdetails?id=R00329743_en); [Experis](https://www.experis.com/en/job/400460/copilot-studio-developer); [Apex Systems](https://www.apexsystems.com/job/3052282_usa/microsoft-copilot-studio--power-platform-developer); [Cognizant](https://careers.cognizant.com/us-en/jobs/44028/microsoft-copilot-studio-developer/); [Jobright](https://jobright.ai/jobs/lead-copilot-developer-jobs-in-united-states)
- ZipRecruiter's "Copilot Developer" listings (Sept 2026) were 20% full-time, 77% part-time, and 3% contract. The title is ambiguous: it mixes GitHub Copilot, M365 Copilot, and Copilot Studio — [ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Copilot-Developer)
- Tenth Revolution Group (Nigel Frank) listing: "Power Platform & Copilot Engineer," Dallas, $110k–$130k. Undated snapshot — [Nigel Frank search result](https://www.nigelfrank.com/microsoft-modern-workplace-solutions-architect-jobs)

**Cross-ecosystem AI premium**
- Lightcast "Beyond the Buzz" (July 2025), covering more than 1.3B postings: postings listing AI skills advertise about 28% (~$18k/yr) higher salaries. Postings requiring 2+ AI skills carry about 43%. 51% of AI-skill postings are outside tech industries, up from 39% in 2019 — [Lightcast press release](https://lightcast.io/resources/blog/beyond-the-buzz-press-release-2025-07-23); [Staffing Industry Analysts](https://www.staffingindustry.com/news/global-daily-news/job-posts-that-include-ai-skills-offer-28-higher-salaries)
- Randstad (Sept 2026 webinar) re-cited the 28% premium but noted pay bands "aren't budging." Employers advertise the premium without restructuring salary bands — [HCAMag](https://www.hcamag.com/us/specialization/transformation/ai-skills-carry-a-nearly-18000-premium-but-pay-bands-arent-budging/594128)

### Inferences
- In the Salesforce ecosystem, AI skill is becoming the price of entry rather than a growth market. Total demand is shrinking, and Agentforce is taking share of it. A JS engineer entering via generic "Salesforce Developer" would face a supply glut (developer supply +20% vs demand −12%, if that 10K figure holds). Entering at the architect or FDE end targets the real gap.
- Microsoft AI-agent demand is harder to measure because it is spread across Power Platform, Azure AI Foundry, M365, and generic "AI engineer" titles. The visible Copilot Studio title market is small and contract-heavy, which suggests Copilot Studio is usually one skill within a broader Power Platform or Azure role rather than a standalone career title.
- "Forward deployed engineer" is the title that best matches a full-stack JS profile. At Salesforce it is a senior role requiring 6+ years.

### Gaps
- No LinkedIn Economic Graph, Indeed Hiring Lab, or Lightcast data was found that breaks out "Copilot Studio developer" or "Agentforce developer" posting growth specifically. There is no Microsoft-ecosystem equivalent of the 10K report.
- No platform-specific AI premium data from a survey source; only one recruiter anecdote (KORE1, Salesforce).
- The search found no data on FDE roles at Microsoft partners or Microsoft itself.

## 2. Market share, installed base, and analyst forecasts/rankings

### Takeaway
Microsoft's distribution base is an order of magnitude larger: over 450M paid M365 commercial seats, of which more than 30M are paid M365 Copilot seats (FY26 Q4). Salesforce dominates CRM at about 20% share versus Microsoft's 4%, and its AI revenue is growing fast from a smaller base. Gartner expects more than 40% of agentic AI projects to be canceled by end-2027, which argues for roles tied to production delivery and governance rather than pilots.

### Cited Findings
- Microsoft FY26 Q3: more than 20M paid M365 Copilot seats. FY26 Q4: more than 30M, with net seat adds more than doubling QoQ. Accenture deployed more than 740,000 seats, Microsoft's "largest Copilot win to date." Over 450M paid M365 commercial seats, so Copilot is roughly 7% penetration; the 7% is an analyst calculation — [Microsoft FY26 Q3 call](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q3); [Microsoft FY26 Q4 call](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q4); [No Jitter](https://www.nojitter.com/ai-automation/microsoft-365-copilot-hits-20-million-paid-seats). The exact Q4 call date conflicts across sources (July vs Sept 2026).
- Copilot Studio: in Q1 FY2026 (Jul–Sep 2025), 160,000 organizations created more than 400,000 custom agents. This is an older figure, from search snippet summaries of Microsoft disclosures — [Microsoft investor relations](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q3)
- Salesforce FY26 (ended Jan 31 2026): Agentforce ARR was $800M, up 169%. 29,000 Agentforce deals had closed (the release says "deals," not "paid deals"). Agentforce + Data 360 ARR was over $2.9B, including $1.1B of Informatica ARR. Over 60% of Q4 AI/Data bookings came from existing-customer expansion — [Salesforce FY26 Q4 release](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/02/25/fy26-q4-earnings/)
- Salesforce Q2 FY27 (ended Jul 31 2026): Agentforce ARR was over $1.5B, up more than 240%, but the definition changed to include Slackbot and Headless 360, so it is not like-for-like with earlier quarters. Agentforce + Data 360 ARR was nearly $3.9B, up more than 210%. 3.2B "Agentic Work Units" were processed in the quarter. About half of Agentforce bookings came from customers replenishing credits. Revenue was $11.35B, up 11% — [Salesforce investor release](https://investor.salesforce.com/news/news-details/2026/Salesforce-Delivers-Record-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027-Results/default.aspx); [Nasdaq call highlights](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/salesforce-q2-earnings-call-highlights)
- Salesforce Ben survey: 85% of surveyed Salesforce customers have tested or deployed Agentforce, but "nearly half" remain in testing or pilot. 30.4% of 2026 Admin Survey respondents named Agentforce as a least-confident area — [Salesforce Ben, Oct 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/agentforce-jobs-are-booming-while-projects-stall-in-pilot/)
- IDC 2025 CRM share: Salesforce 20.0% (from 20.7% in 2024), #1 for the 13th year. Oracle 4.1%. Microsoft fell from 5.2% to 4.0% (#3). Vendor-published summary of IDC — [Salesforce news](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/idc-crm-market-share-ranking-2025/); [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/74831/salesforce-tops-idc-global-crm-market-share-for-13th-straight-year)
- Gartner (June 25 2025): more than 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end-2027, due to escalating costs, unclear business value, and inadequate risk controls. Gartner flags "agent washing" and estimates only about 130 of thousands of "agentic" vendors are genuine. It also predicts that by 2028, 15% of day-to-day work decisions will be made autonomously by agents (from 0% in 2024) — [Gartner press release](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027)
- Forrester Wave, Customer Service Solutions Q1 2026: Leaders are Salesforce, Microsoft, Pegasystems, and ServiceNow. Forrester Wave, Conversational AI for Employee Service Q3 2026: Leaders are Atlassian, Kore.ai, Salesforce, and ServiceNow (Microsoft not listed as Leader per this source). Secondary summaries — [CX Today](https://www.cxtoday.com/ai-automation-in-cx/the-forrester-wave-says-ai-will-run-customer-service-cx-leaders-need-a-new-operating-model/); [CX Foundation](https://cxfoundation.com/blog/forrester-wave-conversational-ai-employee-service)
- Gartner MQ for Enterprise Low-Code Application Platforms 2025 (July 28 2025): OutSystems (Leader, 9th year) and Appian (Leader) confirmed via their press releases. Microsoft's and Salesforce's placements were not confirmed in the sources found — [Business Wire / OutSystems](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250730042263/en/OutSystems-Named-a-Leader-for-the-Ninth-Year-in-Gartner-Magic-Quadrant-for-Enterprise-Low-Code-Application-Platforms-Paving-the-Way-for-Agentic-AI-Innovation); [Appian](https://appian.com/about/explore/press-releases/2025/appian-recognized-as-a-leader-in-the-2025-gartner-magic-quadrant)

### Inferences
- Microsoft's AI-agent work is driven by horizontal distribution. Every M365 tenant is a potential Copilot Studio or agent customer, so long-run job volume is likely higher, but much of it is spread thin: an internal IT generalist adds Copilot Studio to an existing role. Salesforce's AI-agent work is vertical and concentrated in front-office CRM use cases, which tends to produce more specialized, consultancy-delivered roles.
- Copilot penetration is around 7% of the seat base, so Microsoft has a long adoption runway through 2031. Salesforce's AI growth relies heavily on expanding existing accounts (more than 60% of bookings) and on credit replenishment, a sign of real consumption in production.
- Gartner's cancellation forecast implies a 2027–2028 shakeout. Roles tied to pilots and proofs of concept are most exposed. Roles tied to data readiness (Data Cloud/Data 360, Dataverse/Fabric), governance, and integration are least exposed. Salesforce Ben commenters say stalled projects "stall on data and permissions."

### Gaps
- No Gartner or Forrester ranking specifically for "AI agent platforms" comparing Microsoft vs Salesforce was found. Microsoft's and Salesforce's 2025 low-code MQ positions were not confirmed.
- No US-specific installed-base split was found for either vendor.
- No IDC forecast for enterprise agent-platform spend by vendor was found.

## 3. Long-term job security, salary growth, and supply/demand balance

### Takeaway
The evidence points to oversupply at the generic developer and admin layer in Salesforce, and shortages at architect, Data Cloud, and FDE levels. On the Microsoft side, there is no hard supply/demand data, only signs of contract-heavy demand. Neither ecosystem has a published jobs-to-certified-professionals ratio.

### Cited Findings
- Salesforce Technical Architect demand grew 27% vs 4% supply growth; Developer demand fell 12% while supply grew 20% (10K data via secondary sources; not verified to be 2026) — [Fastdolphin (recruiter)](https://fastdolphin.com/blog/hire-agentforce-developer/); [Salesforce Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/agentforce-jobs-are-booming-while-projects-stall-in-pilot/)
- Recruiter view: generalist Salesforce developers are easier to find than the technical architects and Data Cloud consultants who run agents in production — [Fastdolphin](https://fastdolphin.com/blog/hire-agentforce-developer/)
- Salesforce's 2026 layoffs were under 1,000 roles, which a recruiter characterizes as a reshuffle rather than a contraction. Recruiter source — [KORE1](https://www.kore1.com/salesforce-layoffs-2026/)
- The Trailblazer community exceeds 20M and grows every year. This is a supply-side indicator of very large credentialed-talent supply relative to roles — [Salesforce Ben, Oct 16 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforce-job-demand-is-down-13-should-you-still-bother-in-2027/)
- Commenter estimate: a 62% cumulative decline in Salesforce demand since 2021. This is a reader calculation; label it as anecdotal — [Salesforce Ben comments](https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforce-job-demand-is-down-13-should-you-still-bother-in-2027/)
- AI pay premiums are advertised but salary bands are not being restructured (Randstad, Sept 2026) — [HCAMag](https://www.hcamag.com/us/specialization/transformation/ai-skills-carry-a-nearly-18000-premium-but-pay-bands-arent-budging/594128)
- UK market data (ITJobsWatch, 6 months to Jan 4 2026): Power Platform Specialist median £51,000. UK only, included as directional context for Microsoft-side pricing pressure — [ITJobsWatch](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/uk/power%20platform%20specialist.do)

### Inferences
- Salesforce: job security is strongest for "Agentforce + Data Cloud architect" or FDE profiles, and weakest for generic developer or admin roles. The 10K data shows a structural tilt from breadth to AI and data depth.
- Microsoft: low-code Copilot Studio work is likely to commoditize, since Microsoft explicitly targets makers and business users. Pro-code agent work (Azure AI Foundry, M365 Agents SDK, Agent 365 governance) is more defensible and blends into general AI engineering demand, which also offers more exits.
- Over a 2026–2031 horizon, the Microsoft path likely offers more total openings and better diversification. The Salesforce path offers a sharper but narrower premium, at the cost of ecosystem concentration risk. This is a judgment synthesized from the sources above, not a sourced forecast.

### Gaps
- No ratio of open jobs to certified professionals was found for either ecosystem (e.g., Agentforce Specialist holders vs postings, or AB-100 holders vs postings).
- No Tenth Revolution / Nigel Frank / Mason Frank 2026 salary survey was found. No Robert Half 2026/2027 breakout for these platforms.
- Reddit r/salesforce and r/PowerApps practitioner sentiment was not captured in this pass.

## 4. Pricing and licensing trends and their effect on adoption and hiring

### Takeaway
Both vendors have moved to consumption-based credits. Copilot Studio is cheaper per action at list price (about $0.04–0.05 vs about $0.10 for Agentforce) and can be bundled with the $30/user/month M365 Copilot seat. Agentforce has changed pricing models three times. Consumption pricing ties revenue, and therefore partner hiring, to agents actually running in production, rather than to license sales.

### Cited Findings
- Agentforce Flex Credits: $500 per 100,000 credits. A standard action is 20 credits (about $0.10); a voice action is 30 (about $0.15). The legacy model is $2 per conversation, and per-user editions start around $125+/user/month. Agentforce "has cycled through three pricing models." Prepaid credits don't roll over. Third-party summaries — [Supered](https://www.supered.io/blog/agentforce-pricing/); [Macha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/agentforce-pricing-explained); [ExploreAgentic](https://www.exploreagentic.ai/comparisons/agentforce-vs-copilot-studio/)
- Copilot Studio: billed in Copilot Credits. A classic answer is 1 credit, a generative answer 2, and an agent action 5. A 25,000-credit pack is $200/month (annual), or pay-as-you-go at $0.01/credit. An agent action costs about $0.04 (pack) to $0.05 (PAYG). Copilot Studio use is bundled with M365 Copilot at $30/user/month for internal agents — [Macha, Copilot Studio pricing](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/copilot-studio-pricing-explained); [WorkAgent](https://workagent.ai/agentforce-vs-copilot-studio)
- Salesforce Q2 FY27: about half of Agentforce bookings came from customers replenishing usage credits — [Nasdaq call highlights](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/salesforce-q2-earnings-call-highlights)
- Gartner cites "escalating costs" as a primary cause of projected agentic project cancellations — [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027)

### Inferences
- Microsoft's lower unit price and M365 bundling lower the barrier for internal, employee-facing agents. This drives broad but often in-house hiring (IT departments, CoEs). Salesforce's higher per-action price aligns with customer-facing, revenue-tied use cases where ROI justifies cost. This drives SI/consultancy hiring for fewer, higher-value deployments.
- Credit replenishment at Salesforce is a positive signal for production-delivery roles. Repeated repricing signals buyer friction that could slow mid-market adoption.

### Gaps
- Vendor primary rate cards were not fetched this pass; figures come from third-party summaries and should be verified against Microsoft's Copilot Studio licensing guide and Salesforce's Agentforce pricing page.
- No source directly quantifies how pricing changes affected hiring volumes.

## 5. Skill portability and JavaScript/TypeScript leverage

### Takeaway
Microsoft's pro-code agent stack — the M365 Agents SDK in TypeScript/Node with Express hosting, Azure AI Foundry, and Teams/React — is plain Node/TypeScript and transfers more directly to general software engineering. Salesforce's JS surface (LWC, now with full TypeScript support for base components; Heroku) is real but sits inside a proprietary platform alongside Apex and Agent Script. Both now support MCP, which increases portability of agent-integration skills.

### Cited Findings
- The Microsoft 365 Agents SDK has a JavaScript/TypeScript implementation. Packages include @microsoft/agents-hosting, @microsoft/agents-hosting-express, @microsoft/agents-activity, and @microsoft/agents-copilotstudio-client. It supports building multichannel agents for M365, Teams, Copilot Studio, and web chat — [Microsoft Learn: Agents SDK JS reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/javascript/api/overview/agents-overview?view=agents-sdk-js-latest); [GitHub microsoft/Agents](https://github.com/microsoft/agents); [Copilot Studio integration docs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/publication-integrate-web-or-native-app-m365-agents-sdk)
- Microsoft Agent 365 invokes governed MCP servers for M365 workloads (Mail, Calendar, SharePoint, Teams) under admin control. It works with agents built on any SDK or platform, including Copilot Studio and the Agents SDK — [Microsoft Learn: Agent 365 SDK and CLI](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-agent-365/developer/)
- Salesforce Spring '26: LWC gains full TypeScript support for base components, GraphQL mutations, and dynamic event listeners. The Salesforce DX MCP server included a tool to convert JS LWCs to TypeScript. As of August 2026, the LWC MCP tools are superseded by "LWC skills," used through the Agentforce Vibes Extension — [Salesforce Ben, Spring '26 dev features](https://www.salesforceben.com/9-features-for-salesforce-developers-in-the-spring-26-release/); [Salesforce LWC MCP docs](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/lwc/guide/mcp-intro.html)
- Agentforce rich agent UI uses Custom Lightning Types in Agent Script (LWC-based) — [Salesforce Developers blog, May 2026](https://developer.salesforce.com/blogs/2026/05/use-custom-lightning-types-in-agent-script-for-rich-agent-ui)
- Summer '26: Atlas Reasoning Engine 3.0 can call external MCP servers as action sources, and Agentforce workflows can be published as MCP tools. A2A multi-agent orchestration was also added. Third-party blog; verify — [ChatForest](https://chatforest.com/builders-log/salesforce-summer-26-agentforce-multi-agent-orchestration-atlas-a2a-mcp-builder-guide/); [Salesforce Summer '26 dev guide](https://developer.salesforce.com/blogs/2026/06/the-salesforce-developers-guide-to-the-summer-26-release)

### Inferences
- For a full-stack JS engineer, the Microsoft pro-code path (Agents SDK + Azure AI Foundry + Teams apps + PCF/React for Power Apps) uses the existing stack almost unchanged, and the skills transfer to non-Microsoft AI engineering. The Salesforce path requires learning Apex, SOQL, the metadata/DX model, and Agent Script. LWC reuses JS/TS skills, but in a platform-specific component model, so skills transfer less outside Salesforce.
- MCP support on both sides is a hedge: MCP servers written in TypeScript are portable across Claude, OpenAI, Copilot, and Agentforce. Building MCP integration skills is ecosystem-neutral.
- Salesforce's churn of developer tooling (LWC MCP tools superseded within months) suggests fast-moving platform-specific knowledge that depreciates quickly.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on how often pro-code JS/TS is required in Copilot Studio vs Agentforce job postings.
- No current details were confirmed on Heroku AI / Heroku's role in Agentforce, or on Power Pages and PCF demand.

## 6. Recommended learning paths, certs, and time-to-hire

### Takeaway
Microsoft launched an agentic AI cert family in early 2026: AB-100 Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect Expert, plus AB-730/731/900. AB-100 is an architect-level credential that requires an Associate prerequisite. On the Salesforce side, Agentforce Specialist is the entry AI cert, and the gap 10K data identifies is at the architect and Data Cloud level. No credible time-to-hire data was found for either path.

### Cited Findings
- Microsoft AB-100 (Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect Expert). Domains: Plan AI-powered business solutions (35–40%), Design (30–35%), Deploy (20–25%). It requires one Associate-level prerequisite cert. Exam updates are scheduled for November 16, 2026 — [Microsoft Learn: AB-100 exam](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/ab-100/); [Microsoft Learn: certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/agentic-ai-business-solutions-architect/)
- The AB-730, AB-731, AB-900, and AB-100 exams reached GA around January–February 2026; sources conflict on the exact month — [d365training](https://d365training.com/blog/ab-100-exam-guide/); [Examinotion](https://examinotion.com/blog/microsoft-ignite-ai-certification-impact)
- Copilot Studio job postings ask for 2–3+ years of hands-on production experience, and Salesforce FDE postings ask for 6+ years of software or technical delivery — [Apex Systems](https://www.apexsystems.com/job/3052282_usa/microsoft-copilot-studio--power-platform-developer); [Cognizant](https://careers.cognizant.com/us-en/jobs/44028/microsoft-copilot-studio-developer/); [ITPro](https://itpro.com/security/salesforce-doubles-down-on-forward-deployed-engineers-in-uk-agentforce-push)

### Inferences
These are suggested paths synthesized from the findings above, not sourced recommendations.
- **Microsoft path for a JS engineer (2026–27):**
  1. Build with the M365 Agents SDK (TypeScript) and a Copilot Studio agent that calls a custom MCP server or API.
  2. Earn an Associate cert as a prerequisite (e.g., PL-400 Power Platform Developer or AI-102 Azure AI Engineer; names not verified this pass).
  3. Target AB-100 later.
  4. Add Azure AI Foundry deployment experience.
- **Salesforce path for a JS engineer:**
  1. Earn Platform Developer I (LWC + Apex) and the Agentforce Specialist cert.
  2. Add Data Cloud Consultant, since data is where projects stall.
  3. Build a portfolio around LWC/TypeScript + Agentforce actions + MCP.
  4. Aim for FDE or technical architect, where the shortage is. Avoid competing as a generic Salesforce Developer.
- Because postings require 2–6+ years of hands-on platform experience, a career-switcher's realistic route is likely through SI and consultancy partners (Accenture, Cognizant, and similar firms appear heavily in Copilot Studio postings) or contract roles, not direct enterprise hires.

### Gaps
- No credible time-to-hire data was found for either ecosystem.
- Agentforce Specialist cert holder counts and current Salesforce AI cert names and requirements were not verified this pass.
- PL-400 and AI-102 status in 2026 (retired or renamed) was not verified.
