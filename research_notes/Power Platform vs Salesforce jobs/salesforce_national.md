# Salesforce Ecosystem Job Market Outlook (US national), 2026–2031, including AI and Agentforce roles

Research date: 2026-11-09. Most labor-market data here is **global**, from the 10K Salesforce Talent Ecosystem Report as cited by Salesforce Ben (SF Ben). Clean US-only posting counts are scarce. Each figure is labeled with its date and scope.

## 1. Current posting volumes and trend (the 2023–2025 contraction and whether it recovered)

### Takeaway
After a severe 2024 contraction (global demand -37%), demand recovered modestly in 2025 (+8%). The 2026 10K report then shows overall Salesforce demand down again (-13%). Within that, there is a sharp shift *toward* Agentforce/AI and architect roles and *away from* classic cloud (Sales, Service, Marketing) and junior developer/admin work. Supply runs about 3.4x demand, so the market is a reshuffle, not a broad recovery.

### Cited Findings
- **10K 2025 report (global):** Salesforce job-listing demand rose **+8% YoY in 2025** after a **-37% drop in 2024**, while global talent supply rose **+27%**. Supply is about **3.4x demand** ("over 330%" saturation). — [SF Ben, Oct 29 2025](https://www.salesforceben.com/the-state-of-the-salesforce-job-market-in-2025-26-according-to-the-10k-report/)
- **2025 demand vs supply by role (global, 10K):**
  - Admin: demand **+14%**, supply about **+45–47%**. Admins are only **9%** of listings.
  - Developer: demand **-12%** (the only role with declining demand), supply **+20%**.
  - Technical Architect: demand **+27%**, supply **+2–4%**. Architects are about 1% of the talent pool.
  — [SF Ben, Oct 2025](https://www.salesforceben.com/the-state-of-the-salesforce-job-market-in-2025-26-according-to-the-10k-report/); [SF Ben "Careers in 2030", Jul 8 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/what-will-salesforce-careers-look-like-in-2030/)
- **Supply growth history (global):** talent supply grew **28% (2023), 19% (2024), 27% (2025)**. — [SF Ben, Jul 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/what-will-salesforce-careers-look-like-in-2030/)
- **10K 2026 report (global), cited Oct 19 2026:**
  - Overall Salesforce talent demand **-13% in 2026**.
  - By cloud: Sales Cloud **-13%**, Service Cloud **-21%**, Marketing Cloud **-28%**.
  - Agentforce expertise demand **+63% YoY globally** and **+87% in North America**. Agentforce's share of Salesforce listings rose from **5% to 9%**.
  — [SF Ben, Oct 19 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/agentforce-jobs-are-booming-while-projects-stall-in-pilot/)
- **Glassdoor (global, keyword-based):** Salesforce postings rose from **~14,000 (May 2024)** to **31,200 (Sep 2025)**, up 120%+. Caveats:
  - Glassdoor counts include roles where "Salesforce" is only a keyword.
  - Recruiter Mat Roche called the market "pretty flat."
  - Recruiter Mat Roche described admin roles as "disappearing."
  — [SF Ben, ~Aug 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforce-job-postings-more-than-doubled-in-16-months-what-changed/)
- **SF Ben 2025-26 salary survey:** nearly **90%** said the market is harder than in previous years, and **55.2%** saw fewer opportunities in their role. — [SF Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforce-job-postings-more-than-doubled-in-16-months-what-changed/)
- **US snapshot counts (secondary, undated or loosely dated):**
  - LinkedIn: ~**17,000+** "Salesforce Developer"-related US listings on a broad search, ~**4,000+** on an exact title.
  - Glassdoor: ~**2,000** US Salesforce Developer roles.
  - Indeed: reported as anywhere from a few hundred to thousands, depending on filters.
  — [Janbask blog](https://www.janbasktraining.com/blog/salesforce-developer-job-demand/) (low reliability, vendor training blog)
- **Dice:** Salesforce Technical Architect was among roles with **>150% month-over-month growth** in the Dec 2025 Dice Tech Job Report. Overall US tech postings fell **6% MoM** in Dec 2025 (Jan 2026 report). — [Dice Tech Job Report](https://www.dice.com/recruiting/ebooks/dice-tech-job-report) (as summarized in search results; not verified in the full PDF)
- **Indeed US base pay** for Salesforce developers (postings from the past 36 months): average **$120,867**, range $84K–$174K. — [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/career/salesforce-developer)

### Inferences
- The global aggregate demand trend is roughly: 2024 -37%, 2025 +8%, 2026 -13%. That puts 2026 demand well below the 2022–2023 peak. The "Glassdoor doubled" narrative conflicts with 10K's figures and probably reflects keyword inflation.
- North America is the strongest Agentforce demand region (+87%). US demand is therefore likely tilting toward AI/agent and architect skills faster than the global average.

### Gaps
- No authoritative US-only Lightcast or Indeed time series for Salesforce roles in 2023–2026 was found. Lightcast data is paywalled.
- No US-specific Data Cloud (Data 360), MuleSoft, or Tableau posting counts were found.

## 2. IDC "Salesforce Economy" projections, ecosystem size, Trailhead

### Takeaway
The latest IDC study (published Dec 2023, Salesforce-commissioned) projects **11.6M net new jobs globally, 2022–2028**. These are economic-multiplier jobs, not Salesforce-skilled openings. No newer (2025–2026) IDC update and no US breakout was located.

### Cited Findings
- **IDC 2023 study (global, 2022–2028):** **11.6M** net new jobs and **$2.02T** in new business revenue. **4.7M** of the jobs are direct roles in the Salesforce customer base. AI-powered Salesforce clouds are projected to generate **$948B** of revenue in 2028, triple the 2022 figure. — [SF Ben, Dec 16 2023](https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforce-economy-to-create-11-6m-jobs-between-2022-and-2028-says-idc/); [Salesforce Economy page](https://www.salesforce.com/salesforceeconomy)
- **Prior IDC (2021) study:** **9.3M** jobs and **$1.6T** revenue, 2020–2026. TechRepublic questioned how plausible these predictions are. — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/idc-salesforce-economic-impact-study-makes-predictions-that-at-first-glance-seem-impossible/)
- **IDC projections cited by SF Ben in 2025:** $3.34T cumulative digital tech labor spending by 2030, and a $13T cumulative economic impact by 2030. — [SF Ben, Oct 2025](https://www.salesforceben.com/the-state-of-the-salesforce-job-market-in-2025-26-according-to-the-10k-report/)
- **IDC CRM share:** Salesforce has been #1 for **13 straight years**, with **~20.0%** of 2025 CRM revenue (down from 20.7% in 2024). — via [cxfoundation summary, Jun 2026](https://cxfoundation.com/news/salesforce-extends-crm-market-lead) (secondary)

### Inferences
- IDC figures are vendor-commissioned multiplier estimates. They are not useful as a forecast of openings for Salesforce-skilled workers, so use 10K and job-board data for that.

### Gaps
- No US-specific IDC job figure was found for the 2023 study. No updated Trailhead learner count or Talent Alliance placement data was retrieved.

## 3. Salary ranges by role and seniority (US), contractor rates

### Takeaway
2026 US medians run from about **$110K (admin)** to about **$180–210K (technical architect)**. Developers sit around **$120–150K**. Contract rates run about **$75–135/hr** for admins and **$131–208/hr** for technical architects. Recruiter figures (Mason Frank, Stott and May) run 10–20% above SF Ben's survey.

### Cited Findings
All figures below come from one source: [SF Ben "State of Salesforce Salaries in 2026", Aug 12 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/the-state-of-salesforce-salaries-in-2026/). They combine Mason Frank (MF) quartiles, Stott and May (S&M) ranges, and SF Ben survey (SFB) medians from 2,316 respondents.
- **Admin:**
  - MF quartiles: **$75K / $110K / $140.5K**; contract **$75–135/hr**.
  - S&M: $90K / $120K / $150K.
  - SFB: junior $78K / intermediate $92K / senior $109K.
- **Developer:**
  - MF quartiles: **$100K / $150K / $175K**; contract **$98–168/hr**.
  - S&M: $110K / $140K / $170K.
  - SFB: junior $94.5K / intermediate $120K / senior $140K.
- **Functional Consultant:**
  - MF quartiles: $107K / $130K / $156K; contract $105–157/hr.
  - SFB: $75K / $109.5K / $126K.
- **Technical Consultant:**
  - MF quartiles: $105K / $135K / $172K; contract **$120–185/hr**.
- **Business Analyst:**
  - MF quartiles: $90K / $125K / $160K; contract $97–145/hr.
- **Solution/Functional Architect:**
  - MF quartiles: $139K / $170K / $205.5K; contract $121–180/hr.
  - S&M: $150K / $165K / $185K.
- **Technical Architect:**
  - MF quartiles: **$152K / $182.5K / $250K**; contract **$131–208/hr**.
  - S&M has a second set of $180K / $210K / $240K, which the article does not label.
- **Pay structure:**
  - Technical consultants earn about **10–15%** more than functional consultants at the senior level.
  - Median pay roughly doubles from admin to technical architect.
- **Agentforce-focused US roles:** about **$140K–$175K+** (recruiter Kore1, which says production experience is the scarce factor). A ZipRecruiter average for "Salesforce Agentforce" US postings is about **$98.9K**, a broader, lower match. — [Kore1](https://www.kore1.com/salesforce-developer-salary-guide/); [ExamCert](https://www.examcert.app/blog/agentforce-specialist-salary-2026/) (secondary)
- **Admin job changers:** **51%** of admins who changed jobs took a pay cut (SF Ben survey). — [SF Ben definitive guide 2026](https://salesforceben.com/the-definitive-guide-to-salesforce-careers-in-2026) (via search summary)
- **SFCC (B2C Commerce) developers, ZipRecruiter estimates:**
  - About **$110K** for SFCC developer and SFCC front-end.
  - About **$129K** for Commerce Cloud developer.
  - About **$142K** for the broad SFCC category (Aug 2026).
  — [ZipRecruiter SFCC](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/SFCC); [ZipRecruiter SFCC front-end](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Sfcc-Front-End-Developer)

### Inferences
- The pay gap from developer to architect (about +$30–60K median) is the main financial lever, and the 10K data shows architect supply growing only 2–4% against 27% demand growth.

### Gaps
- No verified CTA-specific salary was found in 2026 sources.
- No Glassdoor or Dice Salesforce-specific 2026 salary tables were retrieved.
- The Mason Frank 2026 guide is still in survey; the MF figures come via SF Ben.

## 4. Salesforce corporate signals: revenue, Agentforce, Data Cloud, headcount, AI-hiring statements, Informatica

### Takeaway
Salesforce is growing about 11% a year, and AI/data ARR is scaling fast: combined Agentforce + Data 360 ARR is about **$3.9B**. Inside Salesforce itself, AI is being used to hold engineering flat, shrink support, and redirect hiring to sales. That is a direct signal that AI substitutes for some technical labor.

### Cited Findings
- **Q2 FY27 results (quarter ended Jul 31 2026):**
  - Revenue **$11.35B (+11% YoY)**.
  - Subscription and support revenue $10.8B (+12%).
  - Total RPO $66.3B (+11%); cRPO $33.5B (+14% constant currency).
  - FY27 revenue guidance **$46.1–46.4B (+11–12%)**, including about $200M from the pending **Contentful and Fin** acquisitions.
  — [Salesforce 8-K Ex. 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001108524/000110852426000187/crm-q2fy27xexhibit991.htm)
- **Agentforce and Data 360 ARR:**
  - **Agentforce ARR >$1.5B (+240% YoY)**. From Q2 FY27 this figure includes Slackbot and Headless 360, so the YoY comparison is inflated.
  - **Agentforce + Data 360 ARR ~$3.9B (+210%)**.
  - One summary gives Data 360 ARR as **$2.4B**, including **$1.1B of Informatica Cloud ARR**. This split was not verified in the 8-K.
  — [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001108524/000110852426000187/crm-q2fy27xexhibit991.htm); [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/salesforce-net-income-jumps-87-as-agentforce-and-data-360-arr-surges-more-than-210-to-nearly-3-9-billion/); [Futurum](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/salesforce-q2-fy-2027-can-agentforce-drive-revenue-reacceleration/)
- **Usage, Q2 FY27:**
  - **7.0B** Agentic Work Units delivered to date, **3.2B** of them in Q2.
  - Data 360 ingested **104T records (+355%)**.
  — [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/salesforce-net-income-jumps-87-as-agentforce-and-data-360-arr-surges-more-than-210-to-nearly-3-9-billion/)
- **Revenue segments, Q2 FY27:**
  - "Agentforce Apps" (Sales, Service, Marketing, Commerce, Slack): **$7.19B, +8% constant currency**.
  - "Data 360, Headless Platform and Other" (includes Informatica, MuleSoft, Tableau): **$3.62B, +20% constant currency**.
  - Informatica contributed **$456M** in Q2.
  — [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001108524/000110852426000187/crm-q2fy27xexhibit991.htm)
- **Paid Agentforce deals:** the latest count found was **>6,000 paid deals and >12,500 total**, as of Q2 FY26 (Jul 2025). No newer count was disclosed in Q2 FY27 coverage. — [Digital Commerce 360, Sep 2025](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/09/05/salesforce-revenue-10-billion-q2-fy25/)
- **Informatica:** acquisition closed **Nov 18 2025** for about **$8B** ($25 per share). Salesforce positions it as strengthening Data 360, MuleSoft, and Agentforce 360. — [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4523363-salesforce-completes-acquisition-of-informatica); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/salesforce-snaps-up-informatica-in-usd8bn-deal-to-boost-ai-tools)
- **Headcount:**
  - About **83,334 employees at Jan 31 2026**, up from 76,453 (secondary sources citing the 10-K).
  - Feb 2026: **<1,000** cuts, reportedly including Agentforce and Heroku teams.
  - Jun 2026: 86 cuts reported via California WARN filings (unverified).
  — [AOL/finance](https://www.aol.com/finance/many-employees-does-salesforce-2026-220629030.html); [SF Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforce-lays-off-nearly-1000-employees-in-early-2026-cuts/); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/salesforce-cuts-nearly-1000-jobs-in-a-month-rejig-hits-agentforce-team)
- **Support cuts:** Benioff (2025) said support staff fell from **~9,000 to ~5,000** "because I need less heads," attributing the cut to AI agents. — [ABC7](https://abc7news.com/post/4000-salesforce-customer-service-jobs-cut-due-artificial-intelligence-ceo-marc-benioff-says/17722910/)
- **Engineering:** on the May 27 2026 earnings call, Benioff said engineering headcount has been **"mostly flat" for two years at ~15,000**, crediting coding agents. One outlet reports a **30%+** productivity gain. A secondhand quote: "We're not adding any more software engineers next year." Growth is concentrated in sales, with a target of 3,000–5,000 new salespeople. — [ITPro](https://www.itpro.com/business/business-strategy/marc-benioff-salesforce-software-engineering-hiring-flat-ai); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/salesforce-ai-spending-engineer-hiring-freeze-marc-benioff); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-makers/salesforce-cuts-4000-support-roles-for-ai-but-plans-to-hire-5000-sales-staff-says-ceo-marc-benioff-82829.htm)
- **Customer adoption (SF Ben survey):** **85%** of surveyed customers have tested or deployed Agentforce, but **nearly half** are still in pilot. — [SF Ben, Oct 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/agentforce-jobs-are-booming-while-projects-stall-in-pilot/)

### Inferences
- Core-app growth of about 8% versus about 20% for data, integration, and AI implies that ecosystem job growth will skew toward data, integration, and agent work (Data 360, MuleSoft/Informatica, Agentforce) rather than classic Sales and Service Cloud configuration. This matches the 10K by-cloud demand declines.
- Salesforce's own internal engineering freeze is a leading indicator that customers and partners may also expect more output per developer.

## 5. Certifications and their market value; new AI certs, 2025–2026

### Takeaway
The credential map is being rebuilt around Agentforce:
- AI Associate was retired on Feb 2 2026.
- AI Specialist became Agentforce Specialist.
- 16 certifications are being renamed (most adding "Agentforce") as of Jul 24 2026.
- 24 certifications retire on Feb 1 2027.

Agentforce Specialist appears in postings more often as "preferred" than "required." Production experience is what employers value.

### Cited Findings
- **AI Associate:** retired on **Feb 2 2026**. Candidates are pointed to Agentblazer status and the Agentforce Specialist exam. — [eesel/secondary](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/salesforce-ai-certification); [Trailhead](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/credentials/associateoverview)
- **AI Specialist:** retooled and renamed **Agentforce Specialist**, aligned to the Spring '26 release. — [certcrush](https://www.certcrush.app/blog/agentforce-specialist-certification-2026-cost-pass-mark) (secondary)
- **Retirements and renames:** Salesforce is **retiring 24 certifications on Feb 1 2027** and **renaming 16** as of Jul 24 2026, most adding "Agentforce." A new **Agentforce Life Sciences Consultant** cert launched in Spring '26. — [K2 University](https://k2university.com/news/salesforce-is-retiring-24-certifications-what-to-decide-before-july-24/) (the page returned a 403 on fetch, so this is a search summary only); [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/ai/news/the-agentforce-life-sciences-consultant-certification-0f0abaa4-0ced-4eb2-b24e-5799854cb26f/)
- **Certs in postings:**
  - Insight Global (Michigan) lists **"Agentforce Specialist; PD I/II" as nice-to-have**.
  - Some postings require Agentforce Specialist plus Agentblazer Legend status (e.g., a Walter posting in Germany).
  — [Insight Global](https://jobs.insightglobal.com/find_a_job/michigan/job-550309/)
- **Certs alone are no longer enough** to land entry-level roles. — [SF Ben, Jul 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/what-will-salesforce-careers-look-like-in-2030/)
- **Admin confidence:** **30.4%** of respondents to the SF Ben Admin Survey 2026 named Agentforce as their least-confident area. That points to a skills gap. — [SF Ben, Oct 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/agentforce-jobs-are-booming-while-projects-stall-in-pilot/)

### Inferences
- Value hierarchy, inferred from salary and demand data:
  1. CTA / Application or System Architect (scarce; architect demand +27% vs supply +2–4%)
  2. Agentforce Specialist combined with PD I/II and Data Cloud
  3. PD I/II
  4. Admin (oversupplied)

### Gaps

- Could not confirm whether a distinct "Agentforce Developer" cert or a "Data 360 Consultant" rename exists.

## 6. How AI agents may expand or shrink Salesforce talent demand (5-year view); entry-level saturation

### Takeaway
Expert views split into a bull case and a bear case:
- **Bull:** Salesforce becomes the AI/agent layer for customer operations, which sustains demand for architects and AI-capable developers.
- **Bear:** Third-party agents become the user interface and Salesforce shrinks to a system of record, which shrinks specialist admin and developer roles.

The data so far fits a **polarizing** market: Agentforce, architect, and data demand are up, while junior admin, junior developer, and classic-cloud demand are down.

### Cited Findings
- **SF Ben "Careers in 2030" (Jul 2026):**
  - AI is "the elephant in the room" for developer demand.
  - Routine admin work will be automated.
  - Basic Apex/LWC developers and generic implementation consultants will be squeezed.
  - Architects, and consultants who sell measurable outcomes and compliant AI operations, will grow.
  - It gives both the bull and bear scenarios above. It quantifies no 2030 projection.
  — [SF Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/what-will-salesforce-careers-look-like-in-2030/)
- **Entry level:** "Traditional entry paths are the weakest," and admin supply (+47%) is outrunning demand (+14%). — [SF Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/what-will-salesforce-careers-look-like-in-2030/)
- **Pilots:** Agentforce projects are often stuck in pilot (about half of adopters). Job demand for Agentforce skills is nonetheless booming (+63% globally, +87% in North America). — [SF Ben, Oct 2026](https://www.salesforceben.com/agentforce-jobs-are-booming-while-projects-stall-in-pilot/)
- **Salesforce's own example:** AI cut its support headcount by about 44%, and coding agents kept engineering flat. — [ABC7](https://abc7news.com/post/4000-salesforce-customer-service-jobs-cut-due-artificial-intelligence-ceo-marc-benioff-says/17722910/); [ITPro](https://www.itpro.com/business/business-strategy/marc-benioff-salesforce-software-engineering-hiring-flat-ai)

### Inferences
- Over 2026–2031, total Salesforce-skilled headcount demand is likely flat to modestly down. The mix shifts toward agent design, data unification (Data 360 / Informatica), integration (MuleSoft, MCP/API), governance, and architecture.
- The pilot-to-production gap suggests a multi-year implementation wave for partners and SIs if adoption converts. If it does not, Agentforce demand could deflate like a hype cycle.

### Gaps
- No quantified analyst forecast (Gartner, Forrester, IDC) of Salesforce-specific labor demand to 2030–2031 was found.

## 7. Fit for a JavaScript / full-stack engineer

### Takeaway
A JS/full-stack engineer has real entry points that skip the oversupplied admin path:
- **LWC** (standard web components and JavaScript)
- **B2C Commerce / SFCC** (server-side JS, plus OCAPI/SCAPI and PWA Kit / React)
- **Agentforce actions and APIs/MCP** integrations
- **MuleSoft / integration**

Heroku is a weaker bet given the 2026 cuts there.

### Cited Findings
- **SFCC postings:** ask for **LWC and advanced JavaScript plus OCAPI/SCAPI**. Pay estimates run about **$110K–$142K** (ZipRecruiter, 2026). Posted examples:
  - Manhattan front-end contract: **$114.5K–$133.2K**
  - Atlanta integration role: **$102K–$137K**
  — [ZipRecruiter SFCC](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/SFCC); [ZipRecruiter SFCC Front-End](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Sfcc-Front-End-Developer)
- **Integration and AI demand:** recruiters report rising demand for **integration-focused developers, data roles, and AI roles**, even as generic developer demand falls. — [SF Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforce-job-postings-more-than-doubled-in-16-months-what-changed/)
- **MCP usage:** agentic usage of the platform via **Model Context Protocol calls** surged about sixfold (Q2 FY27 call). That favors API- and agent-integration engineering skills. — [Nasdaq call highlights](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/salesforce-q2-earnings-call-highlights) (via search summary)
- **Heroku:** the Feb 2026 layoffs reportedly hit **Heroku** and Agentforce teams. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/salesforce-cuts-nearly-1000-jobs-in-a-month-rejig-hits-agentforce-team)

### Inferences
- The best-fit target for this profile is "Salesforce developer, LWC + integration + Agentforce actions," stacked with PD I and Agentforce Specialist. It pays a median of about $120–150K and leads toward a technical architect role ($180K+).
- Being a JS generalist is a hedge: if Salesforce demand falls, the skills still transfer.

### Gaps
- No 2026 demand-trend data for Heroku, SFCC posting counts, or MuleSoft developer counts in the US.

## 8. Risks

### Takeaway
The main risks are:
- a saturated entry level (supply about 3.4x demand)
- AI compressing developer and admin work
- Salesforce's own layoffs and its non-sales hiring pause
- customer cost pressure (a 6% list-price increase, Agentforce credit pricing)
- competitors (ServiceNow pushing into CRM; Microsoft is covered elsewhere)

Salesforce still leads CRM share by a wide margin.

### Cited Findings
- **Price increase:** list prices rose **~6% on Aug 1 2025** for Sales, Service, Field Service, and some Industry Clouds (Enterprise and Unlimited editions). — [Constellation Research](https://www.constellationr.com/blog-news/insights/salesforce-raises-prices-across-multiple-products-including-slack); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/salesforce-raises-prices-on-slack-and-more-promises-more-ai-integration-as-a-result)
- **Agentforce pricing:**
  - **Flex Credits** (May 2025) cost $500 per 100K credits, or about **$0.10 per action**. They replaced the earlier $2-per-conversation model.
  - **Agentforce 1 Edition** costs **$550 per user per month**.
  — [The Register, Jun 2025](https://www.theregister.com/2025/06/17/salesforce_ai_prices/); [Constellation](https://www.constellationr.com/node/38972)
- **CRM share:**
  - IDC 2025: Salesforce **~20.0%** (down from 20.7%), Oracle 4.1%, Microsoft about 4.0%.
  - Metrigy puts Salesforce at **25.8%** of 2025 CRM revenue (different methodology).
  - ServiceNow reportedly closed nine CRM deals worth over $1M each in one quarter (newsletter claim).
  — [cxfoundation](https://cxfoundation.com/news/salesforce-extends-crm-market-lead); [Metrigy](https://www.metrigy.com/product/crm-quarterly-market-share-forecast-report-2025-4q25/); [crm.org](https://crm.org/newsletter/servicenow-grabs-nine-crm-megadeals-eyes-market-takeover)
- **Hiring pause:** Salesforce paused hiring outside sales in 2026, while bringing on 1,000 new grads and interns. — [rallies.ai summary](https://rallies.ai/news/salesforce-limits-hiring-to-sales-roles-after-4000-support-cuts-to-boost-ai-efficiency) (low reliability)

### Inferences
- Price increases plus pilot-stage Agentforce could slow new license expansion, which feeds partner and customer hiring. Core-app growth is already only about 8% in constant currency.
- The risk is concentrated in junior admin and junior developer roles. Architect and AI-integration roles look comparatively insulated.

### Gaps
- No HubSpot or ServiceNow CRM share figures comparable to IDC 2025.
- Gartner CRM share data is paywalled.
- No verified 2026–2027 price-increase announcement.
